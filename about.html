About Silver Wolf Stays

Silver Wolf Stays is built on a simple idea: vacation rental owners deserve a hands-on partner as invested in their property's success as they are. We provide remote Airbnb co-hosting and short-term rental management so owners across the US and Canada can save time, deliver exceptional guest experiences, and maximize their property's potential.

The Pack Behind Every Great Stay

Our Story

Silver Wolf Stays was created from nearly 8 years of hands-on experience in the short-term rental industry.

Before building Silver Wolf Stays, our team spent years managing the behind-the-scenes operations that make successful Airbnb and VRBO rentals run — from guest communication and issue resolution to daily operations, owner support, and creating experiences that lead to better reviews.

Throughout those years, we saw a common challenge among property owners: they wanted to grow their rentals, but managing the endless details of guest expectations, operations, and day-to-day decisions became overwhelming. Many did not want a traditional property management company taking over their listing — they wanted a co-host who would work inside their account and leave them in control.

Silver Wolf Stays was built to be the partner we believe every owner deserves — a team that provides the attention, reliability, and care of an in-house manager while giving owners the flexibility of a remote co-hosting solution.

The name Silver Wolf represents how we approach property management: loyalty, teamwork, awareness, and protection. Like a wolf pack, we believe the strongest results come from working together toward a shared goal.

Today, Silver Wolf Stays helps vacation rental owners across the United States and Canada protect their investments, create exceptional guest experiences, and maximize the potential of their properties through hands-on remote co-hosting.

Where We Work

We co-host for vacation rental owners across the United States and Canada. Because our work is remote, your property's location is not a constraint — the owners we work with today range from Beverly Hills, California to Vancouver and Langley, British Columbia.

What matters more than geography is that your property has reliable local cleaning and maintenance contacts. We coordinate those vendors rather than replacing them, and if you do not have them in place yet, we can help you find and vet them during onboarding.

We work primarily on Airbnb and VRBO, and can support Booking.com and direct booking sites as well. In every case we operate as a co-host inside your existing accounts, so your listing, your review history, and your Superhost progress stay yours.
